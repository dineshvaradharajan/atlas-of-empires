# War flags, slice B

Commons description pages and licences checked through the Commons API. Thumbnails are 120 px PNGs in public/flags/, all under 16 KB.

- **mongol-conquests**: none on any side. The Mongol tug (horse-tail spirit banner) was not a flag, and the files for the Mongol Empire, Khwarazm, Jin, Georgia and the Rus' are modern reconstructions. The Mamluk "flag" comes from a later portolan chart (Catalan Atlas, 1375), not from 1260.
- **napoleonic-wars**: France has the tricolour (Flag_of_France.svg, the design in use 1794–1815) and the Napoleonic Kingdom of Italy flag (1805–14, CC BY-SA). The Coalition has the 1801 Union Flag (the war is mostly after 1801), the Russian white-blue-red (the state/merchant flag of the period), the Habsburg black-yellow (the Austrian Empire flag from 1804) and the Kingdom of Prussia flag 1803–1892 (licence: Attribution). Sweden, Portugal, Spain and the German satellites are left out to keep to 4 flags; the Duchy of Warsaw flag is uncertain.
- **paraguayan-war**: the Allies have the Empire of Brazil with 20 stars (1853–1889), Argentina (the sun flag) and Uruguay (since 1830). Paraguay has its 1842–1954 flag (obverse).
- **peloponnesian-war**: none. Greek poleis used shield emblems, not flags. Any Persian standard would be a reconstruction.
- **russian-invasion-of-ukraine**: Ukraine on one side; Russia and North Korea on the other. Western suppliers are left out because they are not combatants. The Wagner Group and the DPR/LPR proxies are left out too.
- **second-punic-war**: none. Roman vexilla and Carthaginian standards survive only as modern reconstructions.
- **seven-years-war**: Great Britain has the pre-1801 Union Flag (1707–1800) and Prussia its 1750–1801 flag (Attribution). France has the royal fleur-de-lis standard (c.1638–1790, CC BY-SA), Habsburg Austria the black-yellow (1700–1806), Russia the white-blue-red and Sweden the blue-yellow cross. Saxony, the Holy Roman Empire and Spain (1762) are left out to keep to 4 flags, as are Hanover and Portugal. The Nawab of Bengal is skipped because no flag of the period is attested.
- **soviet-afghan-war**: the Soviet flag (the 1955–1991 version) and the DRA flag of 1980–1987, which covers most of the war; the Khalq red flag flew only until April 1980. The mujahideen are skipped because there were many factions and no common flag.
- **spanish-american-war**: the 45-star US flag (1896–1908), whose Commons page says it was used in this war. The Cuban Lone Star was flown by the Liberation Army. The Philippine flag of 1898–1901 was Aguinaldo's. Spain has the red-yellow flag (1785–1873, 1875–1931, CC BY-SA).
- **taiping-rebellion**: the Qing yellow dragon flag (1862–1889) is used for the Qing side. It is triangular and dates only from 1862, so it fits just the last two years of a war that ran 1850–64; it is kept because it is the only Qing flag of the period. The Taiping side is skipped because its banners are known only from descriptions and the existing files are reconstructions.
