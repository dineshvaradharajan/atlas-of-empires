## Unverifiable / disputed

- Peak areas for Chola, Vijayanagara (Wikipedia gives c. 880,000 km² for c. 1500 vs. 500,000 here), Goryeo, Joseon, Japan, Majapahit, Pagan, Đại Việt, Ayutthaya, Olmec, Maya, Teotihuacan, Tiwanaku, Wari, Chimú, Thule and Tuʻi Tonga: no Taagepera/Turchin figure; kept, hedged as rough/approximate.
- Taagepera 1979 values (Kushan up to 2.5M, Gupta 1.7M for 440, Qin 2.3M for 220 BCE, Han 6.0/6.5M, Sui 3.0M for 589, Xiongnu, Turks) are cited via Wikipedia; the 1979 paper itself is paywalled and was not read directly.
- Gupta peak: Turchin et al. give 3.5M km² (400) while the lower 1.7M km² (440) is attributed to Taagepera 1979; both now stated.
- Mongol peak: Turchin et al. date 24.0M km² to 1270; Taagepera 1997 gives 23.5M (1294) and 24M (1309). Kept 1270.
- Shang peak year now -1122 because both scholarly sources use the traditional 1122 BCE conquest date; on the modern chronology (end 1046 BCE, as in this profile) that means "late Shang".
- Srivijaya: end year 1377 kept (Majapahit conquest of Palembang), but Wikipedia ends the polity in 1025 or the 13th century; start c. 650 vs. first inscriptions 671–683. Turchin et al.'s 1.2M km² (1200) is generous for a loose port network.
- Chimú rulers: Wikipedia dates Taycanamo c. 1305 and Ñancempinco c. 1375 (flagged there as original research) vs. "c. 900 (legendary)" here; kept the traditional framing as already hedged.
- Göktürk split "around 582": sources give 581–583 for the de facto split and 603 for the formal division; left as is.
- Huayna Capac death: Wikipedia's Inca Empire infobox says 1525, his own article 1527; kept 1527 (matches Taagepera's 1527 peak).
- Langdarma reign: 838 vs 841 start disputed; now shown as "c. 838/841 – 842".
- Gajah Mada as prime minister from 1331 (Wikipedia, citing Pigeaud) — kept; some sources say 1334.
- Olmec start (-1600) and Wari start (600): Olmec dates range from 1600/1500 to 1200 BCE (WHE uses 1200); Wari from c. 450/500 to 600. Kept, as both are within mainstream ranges.
