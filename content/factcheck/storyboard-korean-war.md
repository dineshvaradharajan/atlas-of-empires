# Storyboard: Korean War (`content/cinema/korean-war.json`)

Sides follow `content/wars/d-recent.json`: **0 = South Korea and the UN Command, 1 = North Korea and China**.

## Chapters (8) and why
1. **North Korea invades** (25 Jun – 4 Aug 1950): the invasion, Seoul (28 Jun), Osan (5 Jul), Taejon (20 Jul), the drive to the Naktong.
2. **The Pusan Perimeter** (4 Aug – 15 Sep): the Naktong line, the Naktong Bulge and P'ohang-dong.
3. **Inchon and the breakout** (15 – 30 Sep): Inchon landing, Kimpo, Seoul, Eighth Army breakout, link-up near Osan.
4. **Across the 38th parallel to the Yalu** (1 Oct – 24 Nov): Wonsan, Hamhung, Pyongyang, Chosan on the Yalu, Unsan.
5. **China's offensive and the retreat** (25 Nov 1950 – 24 Jan 1951): Ch'ongch'on, Chosin, Hungnam evacuation, Seoul lost, Line D.
6. **Seoul retaken and the spring offensives** (25 Jan – 30 Jun 1951): Thunderbolt, Chipyong-ni, Ripper, Imjin/Kapyong, the Kansas–Wyoming line.
7. **Talks and the hill battles** (Jul 1951 – Dec 1952): Kaesong/Panmunjom talks, Bloody Ridge, Heartbreak Ridge, Operation Commando, White Horse, Triangle Hill.
8. **The armistice line** (Jan – 27 Jul 1953): Kumsong offensive and the Military Demarcation Line.

## Method
- One continuous **Korean front**, west/south coast → east coast in every key (36 positions plus hold keys every ≤5 weeks through the static 1951–53 period, ~60 keys).
- A separate **Inchon–Seoul beachhead** front, 15–27 Sep 1950.
- Occupied areas are half-planes closed off the front (so they never disagree with it): communist-held South Korea 1950 (keys every 3 days June–Sept, with the Inchon beachhead notched out); communist-held ground south of the 38th parallel 1951–53 (Kaesong, Ongjin); UN-held ground north of the parallel (Oct 1950 onwards, keys every 4 days in the fast phase); UN-held north-east Korea (X Corps), 30 Nov – 24 Dec 1950, shrinking to the Hungnam beachhead.
- Rings and tails are kept off Japan, China and the USSR so nothing is painted on neutral or own ground.

## Uncertainties
- In 1950 the "front" joins separate columns across ground nobody held (e.g. between Eighth Army and X Corps in November); bypassed North Korean units and guerrillas are not shown.
- X Corps' dispersed November positions (Yudam-ni, Hyesanjin, Hapsu, the coast towards Chongjin) are drawn as one area.
- 26–30 Sep 1950: the map sweeps from the perimeter to the 38th parallel; North Korean remnants in the south-west (Mokpo, Kunsan retaken c. 29–30 Sep) are not drawn.
- Lines are accurate to roughly 5–15 km; hill battles of 1951–53 moved the line only a few kilometres.

## Warnings left (tools/cinema/check_history.py)
- Chapter 8 "little visible change": real — the front moved only a few km in 1953 (Kumsong salient).
- Three "pocket" warnings (750–1,760 km² of North Korean ground near 128.6–128.9°E, 38.5–38.7°N, between UN areas): this is the base map's coarse, saw-toothed east coast north of the front's eastern end (Kansong/Goseong), not a real enclave; the front ends correctly on the coast.
