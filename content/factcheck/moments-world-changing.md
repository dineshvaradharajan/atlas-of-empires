## Figures and choices
- Pearl Street customers: the Pearl Street article says 82, Electrification says 85; text says "some 80".
- Smallpox 20th-century deaths: Smallpox article "up to 300 million"; vaccine article "250–500 million". Text uses
  "up to 300 million".
- Aviation article's "9.5 billion passengers in 2024 (ICAO)" looked inconsistent with ICAO's usual totals and
  was not used; the 2017 figure (4.1 billion) from the Airline article was used instead.
- Smartphone article's "75.05 percent of the world population were smartphone users (2020)" was not used;
  ITU's 2025 mobile-phone ownership (82% of people aged 10+, all mobile phones, not only smartphones) was used.
- ChatGPT weekly users (900M, Feb 2026) is a company-reported figure via Wikipedia.
