# War flags, slice C

- **texas-revolution**: Texians none; Mexico = 1823 tricolour with the eagle (Flag_of_Mexico_(1823-1864,_1867-1893).svg). Skipped the Texians: rebel units flew assorted flags (1824 flag, Come and Take It, San Jacinto banner) and the Republic's Burnet flag was only adopted in December 1836, after the war.
- **third-crusade**: all sides empty. Heraldry was barely forming c.1190: the English three lions first appear on Richard I's 1198 seal, the French royal semé-de-lis is not firmly documented before c.1211, and the Jerusalem cross arms and imperial eagle banner of this date are later reconstructions. Ayyubids and Seljuks of Rum had no attested flag of the period.
- **thirty-years-war**: Habsburg side = imperial double-headed eagle banner (1400-1806, CC BY-SA 3.0) and Spain's Cross of Burgundy. Protestant side = Sweden (blue-yellow cross, in use by the 1600s), the French royal standard (blue with gold fleurs-de-lis, Commons dates it from c.1638; France fought from 1635; CC BY-SA 3.0), the Dutch Prinsenvlag (orange-white-blue, the usual flag before the red-white-blue Statenvlag took over), and the Dannebrog for Denmark-Norway. Bavaria, Saxony, Brandenburg and Transylvania were left out: they are lesser members, and no lozenge or other banner is clearly attested as a field flag of the period.
- **vietnam-war**: North = DRV flag (1955-76) and the NLF/Viet Cong half-red, half-blue flag. South = RVN yellow flag with three red stripes, US 50-star flag (1960 on), South Korea's 1949-84 Taegukgi, Australia (1954 design). Thailand, NZ, the Philippines, Laos and Cambodia were left out to keep within four.
- **war-in-afghanistan-2001-2021**: Coalition = US 50-star flag, the Union Flag, and the Islamic Republic's 2004 flag (the 2004-2013 variant). Taliban = the white flag with the shahada (introduced 1997). The Northern Alliance (Islamic State of Afghanistan flag) was left out to keep the Republic's flag. al-Qaeda was skipped: it had no single official flag.
- **war-of-1812**: US = 15-star, 15-stripe flag (1795-1818, the Star-Spangled Banner pattern). Britain = 1801 Union Flag. Indigenous nations were skipped: no attested national flags.
- **wars-of-the-roses**: both sides = the English royal banner (France modern quartered with England, 1406-1603). Henry VI and Edward IV each flew it as king. House badges (the red and white roses, the sun in splendour, Henry Tudor's red dragon) were left out as badges and standards rather than a side's flag.
- **world-war-i**: Entente = Union Flag (British Empire), French tricolour, Russian white-blue-red (the Empire's national flag from 1883/1896), US 48-star flag (1912-59). Central Powers = German imperial black-white-red, the Austro-Hungarian civil ensign (1869-1918, the joint flag), the Ottoman crescent and star (1844-1922), and Bulgaria's tricolour.
- **world-war-ii**: Allies = Union Flag, Soviet flag (1936-55 pattern), US 48-star flag, ROC flag (1928 on). Axis = Japan's Hinomaru (1870 national flag; no Rising Sun war flag), Kingdom of Italy (Savoy tricolour), Hungary (1915-46), Romania (tricolour). Nazi Germany was left out as instructed, with no swastika flag. Vichy = the plain French tricolour, which the French State kept as its national flag.
