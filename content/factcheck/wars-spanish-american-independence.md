## Scope

of the Río de la Plata and Chile (San Martín). Mexico and Central America are left out, so the name says
"(South America)". The war starts with the Caracas junta (19 Apr 1810) and ends with the surrender of Callao
(23 Jan 1826). `moment`: `latin-american-independence` (1808–1826), whose summary already covers these wars.

## Contested facts and how they were handled
- **Casualties:** no reliable total for South America. The main article's infobox gives about 1,000,000 deaths for
  all of Spanish America, 400,000 of them in New Granada (which then included Venezuela); the text says this is one
  estimate for the whole region and that many deaths came from disease, hunger and massacres.
- **Dates:** see the storyboard log (Paracas 7 or 8 Sep 1820; Valencia 3 May 1812; Lima July 1821; Puerto Cabello
  1823; Jamaica Letter September 1815).
- **Execution figure:** "some 7,000 people are estimated to have been executed between 1816 and 1819" is from the
  Spanish reconquest of New Granada article and is hedged as an estimate.
- **Wording:** the decree of war to the death and Boves's appeal to the poor are described as each side's acts and
  aims, without judging the causes.
