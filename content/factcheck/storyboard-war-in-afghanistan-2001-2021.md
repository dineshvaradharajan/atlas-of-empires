# Storyboard: War in Afghanistan 2001–2021 (`content/cinema/war-in-afghanistan-2001-2021.json`)

Sides: **0 = US-led coalition, Northern Alliance and Afghan Republic, 1 = Taliban and al-Qaeda**. Span 7 Oct 2001 – 30 Aug 2021.

## Chapters and why
1. **The 2001 campaign: Kabul and Kandahar fall** (7 Oct – 9 Dec 2001).
2. **Tora Bora** (30 Nov – 17 Dec 2001).
3. **The Taliban return in the south** (Aug 2006 – Dec 2007): Operation Medusa (Pashmul), Musa Qala (seized Feb 2007, retaken 7–12 Dec 2007).
4. **The surge in Helmand and Kandahar** (Jun 2009 – Dec 2010): Panther's Claw, Khanjar, Moshtarak (Marjah, flag raised 25 Feb 2010), Dragon Strike.
5. **Kunduz, 2015**: first provincial capital taken by the Taliban since 2001 (28 Sep), retaken from 1 Oct.
6. **The Doha agreement and the US withdrawal** (29 Feb 2020 – 2 Jul 2021).
7. **The Taliban take over, August 2021** (5–30 Aug 2021).

## Areas and how they were made
- 2001 and 2021 areas are **provinces coloured on the day their main town changed hands** (geoBoundaries ADM1, dilated 3 cells and clipped to the base map's Afghanistan outline on a 0.025° raster; growth tracked with one-day transitions). 2001 dates: Balkh/Samangan 10 Nov, Takhar 11 Nov, Jowzjan/Sar-e Pol/Faryab 12 Nov, the west and Kabul region 13 Nov (Iranian-border provinces "within 24 hours" of Kabul), the east and Uruzgan 14 Nov, Kunduz 25 Nov, the south and Paktia/Khost/Paktika 7 Dec. Tora Bora (an 18 km circle in the Spin Ghar) stays Taliban/al-Qaeda until 17 Dec. 2021 dates come from Wikipedia's table of fallen provincial capitals (Zaranj 6 Aug … Kabul 15 Aug); Panjshir stays out (it held out past 30 Aug). The north and the rest are drawn as two abutting areas so Panjshir is not a hole.
- **Base-map gaps fixed:** the base map keeps the `Taliban` realm until 2005 and only a north-eastern Northern Alliance enclave; the 2001 area (side 0) covers the whole country from 17 Dec 2001 until 31 Dec 2005. From 2006 the base map shows the Republic everywhere, including after the 2021 takeover; the 2021 side-1 areas correct that.
- 2006–2015 strongholds are hand rings: Pashmul (Aug–17 Sep 2006), Musa Qala district (Feb–11 Dec 2007), Marjah (to 25 Feb 2010), Kunduz city and Chahar Dara (Apr–13 Oct 2015).

## Uncertainties
- Province-level colouring is a simplification: in 2001 the countryside changed hands with local commanders; in May–Aug 2021 the Taliban held many rural districts before the capitals fell, which is not mapped (the Doha chapter says so).
- Paktia/Khost/Paktika (2001) are placed with Kandahar on 7 Dec; Baghlan with Kabul on 13 Nov (approximate).
- Zaranj's claim point is moved 3 km east because the base map puts the border town in Iran.
- Several battle figures are claims (Medusa, Musa Qala, Kunduz, Lashkargah).

## Warnings left (0 errors, 3 warnings)
