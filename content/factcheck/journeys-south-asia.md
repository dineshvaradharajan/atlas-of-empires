# Journeys: South Asian kingdoms (November 2026)

Chains chosen, disputed dates and gaps for the 22 journeys in content/journeys/d-south-asia.json. Every source cited was fetched; Britannica returned 403 throughout.


- chera-kingdom: chain Early Cheras -291–305 + Chera Perumals 844–1123, span -291–1123. Gap 305–844: one bridging event (c. 300, Kalabhra-era decline). Sangam rulers (Senguttuvan c. 180, Mantharam c. 215) are dated only by the Gajabahu synchronism, so all are hedged "c.", and their reigns are marked "(c.)". Perumal reigns follow the tentative table on Wikipedia's Chera Perumals page (Rama Kulasekhara 1089–1122; the infobox gives 1124). Kandalur Salai is "around 988". Rama Kulasekhara's retaking of Kollam (c. 1102) is "reportedly". The Muziris papyrus was dropped to stay within 6 sources.
- pandya-dynasty: chain Early Pandyas -291–305 + Pandya Dynasty 592–921 + Pandya Empire 1216–1325, span -291–1325. Events in the gaps: c. 590 Kadungon (just before the 592 stage start) and the c. 250 Kalabhra takeover. Disputed dates: Kadungon 590–620 (Majumdar, Sastri) vs c. 560–590 (Karashima); Srimara's Sri Lanka raid has no firm year (c. 840 is a hedge, "reign of Sena I"); Thirupurambiyam c. 879, other accounts up to 885; Parantaka took Madurai in 910 (not c. 920). Sources disagree on whether Sena II sacked Madurai; I followed the Srimara page, which says he failed to. Jatavarman Sundara I's death is given as 1268 (his page also says 1271).
- satavahana-dynasty: chain Satavahana Dynasty -225–223, span -225–223. There are no events before c. 110 BCE because the summary explains the long chronology (3rd c. BCE) against the short one (late 2nd c. BCE). Reigns follow the recent chronology (Gautamiputra c. 60–84, Pulumavi 84–119, from Ollett/Bhandare via Wikipedia; Sen for Vashishtiputra Satakarni). Nahapana's defeat is placed at c. 78, and the detail notes that c. 124 and the older reign dates of c. 106–130 are disputed. Simuka's reign -125 to -100 is inferred from the Kanaganahalli "year 16 ≈ 110 BCE" record. The Satakarni whom Rudradaman defeated is not named (identity disputed). Only 3 sources: Britannica 403s and the Naneghat page 404s.
- pallava-dynasty: chain Late Pallava Empire 283–897 (the extra layer continues past Cliopatria's 805), profile pallava-empire, span 283–897. Samudragupta's campaign is hedged "c. 350" (his page gives only his reign, c. 335–375). Simhavishnu is c. 575–600 per the Pallava page (the profile says c. 555–590). The date of Tellaru is uncertain: the Nandivarman III page says 830, before his c. 846 accession, so the event sits at c. 846 and the detail flags this. Vikramaditya II's capture of Kanchi is c. 742 (Narwan plates, 741/742). There is no separate Thirupurambiyam event, because it is covered in the Pandya journey.
- kadamba-dynasty: chain Kadamba Empire 353–554, span 353–554. Mayurasharma's founding (c. 345) comes before the realm's first year, so the first event sits at 353 and its text says "c. 345", and his reign is clipped to 353–365 with the label "(r. c. 345–365)". Reigns follow the Kadamba page's text, not its sidebar (e.g. Ravivarma 485–519 vs 497–537, noted in the detail). The Halmidi date is c. 450 (some say the 6th c.). The Skandagupta marriage is "according to one historian" (Desai). Kirtivarman I's annexation of Banavasi (after 566) is outside the span and is mentioned only in the 543 detail and the summary.
- All: details are 82–98 words, texts are 90 characters or fewer, and years ascend inside the spans. No reader-facing text mentions the map or how the atlas is built. Validated with validate_south_early.py.


- vakataka-dynasty: chain "Vakataka Kingdom" 260–510, span 260–510. Cut at 510 to follow content/corrections.json, which clips the realm at [260, 510] (the dynasty faded after Harishena); the index runs to 554. Disputed: founding c. 250, earlier than the first index year 260, so the first event is placed at 260 with "c. 250" in its text. Harishena's dates: the dynasty genealogy gives c. 475/480–500/510, Spink (Ajanta Caves article) 460–477; both are given in the event detail and in the reign name. Pravarasena II and Damodarasena are treated as one king, as in the article's body text. The "Rudradeva" of the Allahabad pillar is hedged. Gap: the end (two unnamed kings, Kalachuri or Kadamba conquest) is obscure.
- chalukyas-of-badami: chain "Chalukya Dynasty" 555–753, span 555–753 (dynasty ended 753; index rows run to 756). Pulakeshin I's founding of Vatapi in 543 comes before the index's first year, so it goes in the summary and the 578 event. Disputed: Pulakeshin II's accession c. 609–611; the war with Harsha c. 618–619 (some date it to the 630s); Badami's fall in 642 (or 642–643); Vikramaditya II in Kanchi after 734 (Narwan plates 741/742), placed at c. 740. The 642–655 Pallava occupation is one reign entry.
- rashtrakuta-dynasty: chain "Rashtrakuta Dynasty" 755–982, span 755–982. Dantidurga's victory in 753 is before the index start, so it is mentioned in the 755 event text. Ends with Indra IV's death on 20 March 982; the index runs to 989 with a tiny residual area. Tailapa II's takeover is dated 973. Disputed: Amoghavarsha's accession 814/815; no date is given for the move to Manyakheta; Kannauj held "until c. 916"; Takkolam 948–949. Gap in reigns: Amoghavarsha II (929–930) and Amoghavarsha III (936–939) are left out.
- western-chalukya-empire: chain "Western Chalukya Empire" 980–1187, span 980–1187 (matches the index). Tailapa's 973 takeover and the 1189 exile of Someshvara IV fall outside the span, so they are covered in the first and last event details and the summary. Disputed: Munja executed "mid-990s" (event at c. 995); Koppam 28 May 1052 (sometimes 1054); Kalyani capital c. 1042; Someshvara IV's restoration c. 1183 (reign table says 1184). The Tailapa III table dates (1151–1162) conflict with the 1149 Kakatiya defeat, so reigns follow the table.
- eastern-ganga-dynasty: chain "Eastern Ganga Kingdom" 1056–1351, span 1056–1351 (matches the index; the dynasty runs c. 493–1434). Disputed: Chodaganga's accession 1077/1078 and death 1147/1150; Utkala conquest c. 1112 (grant of 1118); Jagannath temple begun after 1112 (inscription 1134–35), finished c. 1150 or 1161; Katasin 1243/1244; Kakatiya push north is "c. 1230s". Gap: no sourced dated events between 1264 and 1351, so the last event is 1264 and its detail sums up the decline. The 1324 Tughluq raid on Jajnagar could not be confirmed in any fetched page and was left out. Narasimha Deva III's reign is cut at 1351 (he ruled to 1352).

- **hoysala-empire**: chain "Hoysala Kingdom" 1111–1346 (realm data runs to 1351; cut at 1346, when the kingdom merged into Vijayanagara). Disputed dates: Talakad c. 1116 (Vishnuvardhana's own article gives no date); independence c. 1192 (Ballala II article) vs 1193 (Hoysala Empire); Soratur c. 1191 vs "late 1180s" (Seuna article); Kannanur capital c. 1225 vs after 1235; Ballala III's death 8 Sep 1342 vs 1343. All hedged in the text. No gaps.
- **kakatiya-dynasty**: chain "Kakatiya Dynasty" 1177–1323 (data runs to 1325; the dynasty ended 9 Nov 1323). Independence c. 1163 falls before the realm's first year, so it appears only in the summary and in the first event's detail. Disputed: Rudradeva's death c. 1195 and whether Jaitugi killed him; Mahadeva's death 1198–99 (the Seuna article gives the invasion as c. 1194); Rudrama's reign 1262–1289 vs the older 1261–1295; Prataparudra's death (possibly suicide). Upparapalli (1303) and Rudrama's death (1289) have no place in the sources, so their lonlat is null.
- **seuna-yadava-dynasty**: chain "Yadava Dynasty" 1188–1317. The realm data runs to 1325, but corrections.json treats 1318–1325 as Delhi territory and the kingdom ended in 1317. Disputed: Simhana II's accession 1200 vs 1210; Malik Kafur's campaign 1307 vs 1308; the date of annexation (1317 in the body and infobox, 1308 in the lead). Founding c. 1187 is before the realm's first year, so it is mentioned only in the summary and a detail. Dnyaneshwari c. 1290 has lonlat null.
- **vijayanagara-empire**: chain "Vijayanagara Empire" 1344–1571, profile "vijayanagara-empire"; the summary and reigns agree with the profile (founded 1336, Krishnadevaraya 1509–1529, Talikota 1565, end 1646). The 1336 founding and the 1572–1646 Aravidu period fall outside the realm's years, so they appear only in the summary and in details. Disputed: Madurai conquest c. 1370 (Bowman) vs formal annexation 1378 (used 1378); Firuz treaty 1406 vs 1407; the Bidar move date. Pangal (1406/1420) and the Saluva coup have lonlat null.
- **bahmani-sultanate**: chain "Bahmani Sultanate" 1352–1491. The 1347 founding and the 1518/1527 break-up fall outside the realm's years, so they appear in the summary and the 1490 detail. Disputed: Bidar as capital 1429 (main article) vs 1432 (Ahmad Shah I) vs c. 1426 (Deva Raya II); Ahmad Shah I's death 1435 vs 1436; Adoni 1368 as the first use of firearms (Iqtidar Alam Khan dissents); conquest of Warangal c. 1425. The exact location of Pangal is uncertain, so lonlat is null.

- harsha-empire: chain "Harsha's Empire" 612–655 (span 612–655; Harsha crowned 606, before realm coverage). Disputed: Narmada battle winter 618–19 (some put it later); Shashanka's death c. 636 vs 625; Kannauj/Prayag assemblies only "c. 643" (Xuanzang in India c. 630–644, back in Chang'an 645); Chabuheluo dated 648 (some sources 649); no fetched page confirmed a 641 embassy, so it is described undated. Gap: only 2 reigns (Harsha, usurper Arunasva), because no other ruler falls inside the span; 9 events; nothing happens after 648, but the realm still has area rows to 655.
- gurjara-pratihara: chain "Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty" 732–1036 (realm runs to 1039; the dynasty ends with Yasahpala, d. 1036). Disputed: Arab wars "c. 738" (the place where Nagabhata I fought is unknown; Navsari 738–39 was a Chalukya victory); capture of Kannauj and the battle of Munger "c. 810–816" (Tripartite Struggle page gives 816, but a Rashtrakuta Baroda inscription of 812 already records it); Krishna II at Ujjain "c. 880 / before 888"; Indra III held Kannauj "until c. 916"; Trilochanapala (1018–1027) and Yasahpala (1024–1036) overlap, so Yasahpala is entered from 1027. corrections.json says the empire under Bhoja and Mahendrapala reached Bihar and Bengal, wider than the realm area rows show for those years.
- pala-empire: chain "Pala Empire" 750–1162 (realm runs to 1168; Madanapala's reign ends c. 1162, and Govindapala in Gaya is of uncertain link). Reign dates vary widely by historian (Majumdar, Sircar, Chowdhury, Sinha); Sircar's figures are used for Dharmapala and Devapala (775–812, 812–850) so that they agree with the Munger date. Disputed: Chakrayudha's installation "c. 791" (790–793); Mahipala I's accession 988 vs 978; Rajendra Chola's raid 1021–1023; Kaivarta revolt 1070 vs 1075–1082; Kamboja takeover under Gopala II/III or Vigrahapala II; Ramapala 1077–1130 vs 1069–1122. Gopala numbering (II vs III, III vs IV) differs between sources. Gaps: 850–854 (Vigrahapala I and Shurapala are omitted) and 1036–1038 between reigns. The c. 1153 event also draws on Wikipedia: Vijaya Sena (cited in the Sena journey; the Pala list is capped at 6 sources). corrections.json notes that the Pala court-assembly kings (Avanti, Madra, Gandhara) kept their own lands.
- sena-dynasty: chain "Sena Dynasty" 1072–1230. The realm starts in 1072, and corrections.json clips it to 1070–1230, so Samanta Sena (c. 1070–1095) enters at 1072. Disputed: Vijaya Sena 1096–1159/1160; Gauda taken after Madanapala's 8th year (1152–53), entered as "c. 1153"; Ballala Sena 1159–1179 vs 1160–1178; Lakshmana Sena 1178/1179–1206; Nadia raid 1203–04, Ghurid conquest 1205; "18 horsemen" is hedged as "it is said"; the end c. 1230, though the Tabaqat-i-Nasiri suggests descendants ruled until 1245/1260. Kulinism is flagged as doubtful.
