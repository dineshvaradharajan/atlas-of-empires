# Fact-check log: eras/e-recent.json (2008 and 2020 chapters)

New chapters: **2008 "The Great Crash"** and **2020 "Pandemic and War"** (text runs to the end of 2024). Every page below was fetched (User-Agent "AtlasOfEmpires/0.1 (educational map)", about one request every 2 s) and the claim was checked against its text. Wikipedia statements were used where cited there. The `sources` array of each chapter lists the main pages; the full list is here.

## Map links (powers)
`clio` names were checked against `public/data/clio/index.json` for 2008 and 2020, and against what `tools/content.py` actually resolves (it recomputes `clio` from `name`; `map` is dropped because there is no `data/names/2008.txt` or `2020.txt`):
United States of America, People's Republic of China, (Russian Federation), Japan, Federated Republic of Germany, Kingdom of Great Britain (Cliopatria's name for the UK in these years), French Fifth Republic (2008 only), Republic of India, Republic of Brazil (2020 only). The UK power is named "United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland" and France "French Fifth Republic" so the resolver picks the right realm; a plain "United Kingdom" resolves to the USA and "France" to nothing.

Note for maintainers (outside this file's scope, not changed): the existing 2000 chapter in d-modern.json resolves its "United Kingdom" power to `United States of America` and "France" to `None`. The 1994 chapter has the same France problem.

## Uncertain / softened
- 2008 powers: US "roughly a quarter of world output" and Japan "second-largest economy" (2008), Japan "third-largest" (2020), China "second-largest economy and largest manufacturer" (2020): standard nominal-GDP rankings, not checked against a fetched page.
- India "over 1.1 billion" (2008), China "over 1.3 billion" (2008) and "1.4 billion" (2020), Brazil "some 210 million" (2020): round census-era figures, not fetched.
- Kenya 2008: written "over a thousand people"; estimates range 800–1,500.
- Tigray war: written "by some estimates hundreds of thousands die"; tolls are disputed (independent scholars 385,000–600,000; other estimates much lower).
- Gaza: casualty figure attributed to Gaza's health ministry ("more than 40,000 Palestinians by late 2024"); Israel disputes the ministry's breakdown. October 7 toll given as "about 1,200" and hostages "some 250" (251).
- Sudan: "over ten million" displaced, deliberately below the 14 million in the current article, which counts to 2025–26.
- COVID: "more than 7 million deaths officially reported" and WHO's "almost 15 million" excess deaths for 2020–21. Both are estimates.
- Background items in Wikipedia year pages were not checked separately: Delta wave in India (2021), Scholz succeeding Merkel (Dec 2021), Xi's third term (Oct 2022), Thai youth protests (2020), Nusantara capital project, Canada's truckers' convoy (2022), the Floyd protests, Petro's election (2022), Boric's election (Dec 2021 run-off), Somare as PNG prime minister (2008), Kyrgyz–Tajik clashes in 2021, and the 2011/2014 background events.
