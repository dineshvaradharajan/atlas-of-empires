# Journeys log: slice C, early-modern and modern empires (`content/journeys/c-modern.json`)

Method: every chain realm was checked against `public/data/clio/index.json`. Each chain entry lies inside the realm's years and has area rows for at least 80% of its from–to range. Each span lies inside its chain's coverage, and events and reigns fall inside the span. Britannica returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so the sources are English Wikipedia articles and lists, each fetched and checked for the dates used. `content/corrections.json` notes for Ming, Qing, Ottoman, Maratha and British Raj were read and respected. Where the map starts after the true founding, the span starts with the map. In that case the summary gives the real founding date, and the first reign is clipped to the span with its true dates written in its name (e.g. "Osman I (r. c. 1299–1324)"). The last reign of an empire that ended before its ruler did is clipped the same way.

Overlapping chain entries: the Napoleonic and German journeys list extra realms whose years overlap the main realm, for satellites and colonies. The main realm comes first, so a renderer that draws only the first matching entry still shows the core empire.

## Per empire
- **ottoman-empire**: `Ottoman Empire` 1305–1922. Cliopatria starts in 1305; founding c. 1299 (hedged). The map peaks in 1595 (4.26M km²), below the scholarly c. 5.2M km² for 1683 noted in corrections.json. Baghdad's capture is dated 1535 in the article (other accounts give 1534), so it was left out of the events. 20 of 36 sultans listed.
- **ming-dynasty**: `Ming Dynasty` 1375–1644 → `Southern Ming` 1645–1662. Proclaimed 1368, but Cliopatria starts in 1375. Southern Ming has no rows for 1659–61 and is tiny in 1653–58. The journey ends with Yongli's execution in 1662. Its 1662 row is the start of the Zheng regime on Taiwan.
- **safavid-empire**: `Safavid Dynasty` 1502–1736. Enthroned at Tabriz 22 Dec 1501, but the map starts in 1502. Areas for 1727–37 are near zero (the Afghan occupation and the puppet restoration). Abbas I's accession is 1587 (crowned 1588). Baghdad's recapture is "1623–24" (the article gives both years).
- **mughal-empire**: `Mughal Empire` 1529–1857. Panipat (1526) comes before the map's first year, so Babur's reign is clipped. The map shows the Suri interregnum (1540–55) as a collapse to about 0.3M km². The map peaks in 1700–05 (3.76M km²); Wikipedia gives c. 4.0M km² for c. 1690. The years of the Bijapur conquest (1686) and the Ghaghra battle (1529) could not be confirmed in the fetched text, so they were left out.
- **spanish-empire**: `(Personal union of Crown of Aragon with Crown of Castile)` 1492–1515 → `(Spanish Empire)` 1516–1872 → `First Spanish Republic` 1873–76 (fills the group's 1873–76 gap) → `(Spanish Empire)` 1877–1931 → `Second Spanish Republic` 1932–38 → `Francoist Spain` 1939–75. The 1492–1515 stage shows the Iberian crowns, with little or nothing of the early Caribbean holdings. Wikipedia's infobox gives the leaving of Spanish Sahara as 1976, so the journey ends at the Madrid Accords (14 Nov 1975) with "withdrawal 1976".
- **portuguese-empire**: `Kingdom of Portugal` 1415–1501 → `(Portuguese Empire)` 1502–1910 → `Portuguese Republic` 1911–25 → `Estado Novo` 1926–75. During the Iberian Union (1580–1640) the group drops to about 0.04M km², because the map assigns the lands to Spain. After Brazil (1822) only coastal Africa and Asia remain until the 1890s. The map peaks in 1820 (8.2M km², Brazil). The span ends in 1975; the Macau handover (1999) is mentioned only in the summary. The Macau founding date (1557) could not be confirmed, so it was left out.
- **dutch-empire**: `(Dutch Republic)` 1582–1795 → `(Batavian Republic)` 1796–1810 → `(Netherlands)` 1811–1975. Gaps: the Republic group peaks at only 0.65M km² and does not seem to draw most VOC holdings on Java. `(Netherlands)` has no rows for 1943–44 (the Japanese occupation). From 1950 it shows only the home country, so Dutch New Guinea (to 1962) and Suriname (to 1975) are not drawn, although they appear as events.
- **russian-empire**: `Grand Principality of Moscow` 1462–1546 → `Tsardom of Russia` 1547–1720 → `Russian Empire` 1721–1916 → `Russian Republic` 1917. Starts with Ivan III, where the expansion story begins. Yermak's defeat of Sibir is hedged as "c. 1582" (the article says he was hired in 1581). The map peaks in 1900–04 (23.1M km²); Wikipedia gives c. 22.8M km² for 1895.
- **qing-dynasty**: `Later Jin Dynasty` 1619–1644 → `Qing Dynasty` 1645–1911. This matches the corrections.json note that the map draws 1619–44 as Later Jin. The Qing name dates from 1636. The abdication (12 Feb 1912) falls after the map's last year and is mentioned in the 1911 event. The map's peak (12.5M km²) is below the scholarly c. 14.7M km² for 1790, which the journey uses.
- **maratha-empire**: `Maratha Empire` 1677–1818. The map has no polygon for 1691–1717 (noted in corrections.json) and draws the 18th-century sphere much smaller than the c. 2.5M km² for 1760 (Turchin et al.). Shivaji's coronation (1674) comes before the map's first year. Reigns run as Chhatrapatis to 1713, then Peshwas, because the Peshwas were the effective rulers. Shahu's accession is given as 1707 here (other sources 1708). The end of the Third Anglo-Maratha War is given as 1818 (Baji Rao II surrendered 3 June 1818; one infobox has 1819).
- **napoleonic-empire**: `(First French Empire)` 1805–1814, plus satellites: `Italian Republic` 1805–13 (the Napoleonic Kingdom of Italy, drawn under the earlier republic's name), `Helvetic Republic` 1805–13 (Switzerland under the Act of Mediation), `Kingdom of Naples (Napoleonic)` 1806–14, `Batavian Republic` 1806–10 (the Kingdom of Holland), `Confederation of the Rhine`, `Kingdom of Westphalia` and `Duchy of Warsaw` 1809–13. These three realms only start in 1809 in Cliopatria, although they were founded in 1806–07. There is no existing profile (`french-colonial-empire` covers all French colonial history), so `profile` is null. The empire was proclaimed in 1804 and Napoleon returned in 1815, but the map covers only 1805–14, so both dates appear only in the summary. Only one reign entry, Napoleon I. Spain under Joseph Bonaparte has no satellite realm.
- **british-empire**: `English Colonial Empire` 1609–1705 → `(British Empire)` 1706–1997. The group drops from 27.5M to 15.2M km² in 1932, apparently because the Dominions are no longer counted after the Statute of Westminster (1931). Before 1757 it is small, and Jamestown (1607) comes before the map's first year. The map peaks in 1918 (29.3M km²); Wikipedia places the territorial peak in 1921. Elizabeth II's reign is clipped at 1997.
- **empire-of-japan**: `Empire of Japan` 1868–1945. The map peaks in 1943 (7.04M km²); Wikipedia gives c. 7.4M km² for 1942. Only 3 reign entries (emperors), as there are no other monarchs in this period. The empire formally ended with the 1947 constitution, but the span stops at the 1945 surrender, where the map stops.
- **german-empire**: `German Empire` 1871–1918, plus `German Africa` 1884–1918 for the colonies. The map does not draw Kiautschou or the Pacific colonies. The German Empire's large 1918 polygon (1.42M km²) includes lands in the east taken after Brest-Litovsk, consistent with Wikipedia's 1,501,386 km² for 1918. Profile linked is `germany`, the only profile that covers this empire. Only 3 reign entries (emperors).
- **soviet-union**: `Union of Soviet Socialist Republics` 1922–1991. The map peaks in 1946 (24.6M km²), probably including occupied zones; Wikipedia gives the area as 22.4M km². Leaders follow Wikipedia's list of de facto leaders (Malenkov 1953–55, Khrushchev 1955–64). The intervention in Afghanistan is dated 1979 (the infobox gives 25 Dec 1979).

## Summary
15 journeys, none with an empty chain. All chain names exist in index.json, and ids, spans, events (8–15 each), reigns and sources (3–6 each) pass validation. Reign counts are below 5 for the Napoleonic empire (1), Japan (3) and Germany (3), because these empires had too few monarchs.
