# Fact-check log: eras/c-earlymodern.json (1492–1800)

## Corrections
- [1492] powers "Crown of Castile and Aragon (Spain)" extent: "and the Canary Islands" → "and most of the Canary Islands" — La Palma (1492–93) and Tenerife (1494–96) were not yet conquered — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquest_of_the_Canary_Islands
- [1500] regions.europe: "opening decades of Italian Wars" → "renewing the Italian Wars begun in 1494" — the wars began with Charles VIII's 1494 invasion (the chapter's own events list says so); Louis XII took Milan in Oct 1499 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Wars
- [1500] regions.northern-north-america: "in 1500 the Portuguese Corte-Real brothers explored Greenland and Newfoundland" → "in 1500 and 1501 the Portuguese Gaspar Corte-Real explored Greenland and Newfoundland or Labrador" — in 1500 Gaspar sailed alone and reached only Greenland; the Newfoundland/Labrador landfall was 1501; Miguel sailed only in 1502 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaspar_Corte-Real
- [1530] regions.europe: "Charles V ruled Spain, the Low Countries, Naples, Sicily and Milan," → "Charles V ruled Spain, the Low Countries, Naples and Sicily, controlled Milan through its Sforza duke," — Francesco II Sforza was duke of Milan until 1535; direct Habsburg rule came only after his death — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_II_Sforza
- [1530] regions.southern-africa: "The Luba and Lunda states were forming in the southern Congo savanna" → "The Luba kingdom may already have been taking shape in the southern Congo savanna" — the Lunda state dates to the 17th century; Luba origins are disputed — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Lunda ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luba_Empire
- [1530] regions.north-central-asia: "real power held by his nephew Ubaydullah" → "real power held by Shaybani's nephew Ubaydullah" — Ubaydullah was Muhammad Shaybani's nephew, not Kuchkunji's (Kuchkunji was a son of Abu'l-Khayr, a generation above Shaybani) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuchkunji_Khan
- [1600] overview: "which ruled perhaps 100 million people" → "which ruled well over 100 million people" — estimates for c. 1600 run from about 115 to 150 million — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mughal_Empire
- [1600] regions.southern-north-america: "Creek, Pueblo and Comanche" → "Creek, Pueblo and Apache" — the Comanche split from the Shoshone only c. 1700 and first appear in Spanish records in 1706; Apache were present by 1541/1598 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apache
- [1650] regions.southern-north-america: "Creek, Comanche and Pueblo" → "Creek, Apache and Pueblo" — anachronism, as above — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comanche
- [1650] regions.north-central-asia: "Khabarov was advancing up the Amur" → "Khabarov was campaigning along the Amur" — his 1649–53 campaigns went up, then down, then back up the river — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerofey_Khabarov
- [1700] regions.southern-africa: "Kongo was still recovering from decades of civil war" → "Kongo was still split by the civil war that began in 1665, which Pedro IV would end in 1709" — the Kongo Civil War lasted until 1709 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kongo_Civil_War
- [1700] regions.southern-north-america: "and was setting up missions in Texas" → "and had briefly planted missions in east Texas (1690 to 1693)" — the east Texas missions were abandoned in 1693 and refounded only in 1716 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_missions_in_Texas
- [1700] regions.southeast-asia: "through its wars of succession" → "by intervening in its revolts and succession disputes" — the First Javanese War of Succession began only in 1704; in 1700 VOC leverage came from its Trunajaya-revolt intervention (1677–80) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mataram_Sultanate
- [1715] regions.southern-north-america: "pirates such as Blackbeard were at their peak" → "the golden age of piracy, soon to include Blackbeard, was beginning" — Blackbeard was active as a pirate only from 1716 to 1718 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackbeard
- [1783] regions.southern-africa: "and in Madagascar Andrianampoinimerina was beginning to unite the Merina" → "and in Madagascar the Merina highlands, still divided, would be united by Andrianampoinimerina from about 1787" — he took power c. 1787 and unified Imerina in the 1790s — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrianampoinimerina
- [1783] regions.middle-east-south-asia: "ruled Bengal, Bihar and the Madras and Bombay coasts" → "ruled Bengal and Bihar and held territory around Madras and Bombay" — on the west coast the Company held only Bombay, Salsette and nearby islands — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay_Presidency
- [1783] regions.north-central-asia: "ruled the new province of Xinjiang" → "ruled the conquered region, called Xinjiang (\"New Frontier\"), through a military governor at Ili" — Xinjiang was a military governorate (General of Ili, 1762) and became a province only in 1884 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xinjiang_under_Qing_rule
- [1783] regions.east-asia: "with a population of around 300 million" → "with a population nearing 300 million" — the 1791 registers counted c. 303 million, so 1783 was somewhat below that — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_history_of_China
- [1783] regions.australasia: "Britain would send the First Fleet to found a penal colony at Sydney in 1788" → "Britain's First Fleet would found a penal colony at Sydney in 1788" — the fleet sailed in 1787 and founded Sydney in January 1788 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Fleet
- [1800] regions.southern-africa: "including the Mthethwa under Dingiswayo and the Ndwandwe" → "including the Mthethwa, soon to be led by Dingiswayo, and the Ndwandwe" — his reign is usually dated c. 1806–1817 (some give c. 1800) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dingiswayo
- [1800] regions.northern-south-america: "was exploring Venezuela and the Andes" → "was exploring Venezuela and the Orinoco" — in 1800 Humboldt explored the Orinoco and went to Cuba; he reached the Andes (Bogota) only in July 1801 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt

## Unverifiable / disputed
- [1800] Zaman Shah as Durrani ruler: sources differ on whether Mahmud Shah deposed him in 1800 or on 25 July 1801. Kept, since Wikipedia gives 1801 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahmud_Shah_Durrani
- [1800] Dingiswayo's accession date (c. 1800 vs 1806): softened as noted above.
- [1530] Kuchkunji Khan died in 1530 or 1531 (sources differ). Kept as nominal khan.
- [1530] Luba state origins: estimates run from before the 15th century to the 18th. Softened to "may already have been taking shape".
- [1783] "around 80,000 Africans a year" at the slave-trade peak: widely quoted for the 1780s, but I found no fetchable primary SlaveVoyages aggregate. Kept as is.
- [1492] "Ming China, the richest and most populous state on Earth": a mainstream view, but no single figure was checked.
- [1700] "three emperors ruled more people than all of Europe combined": consistent with Qing (c. 150M+) plus Mughal (c. 150M) estimates against Europe's c. 100–120M. Not verified against one source.
- Britannica pages returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher, so English Wikipedia was used throughout. Rulers I checked against reign lists (Mamluk, Ethiopian, Kazakh, Kongo, Ayutthaya, Toungoo, shogun, Zand) all matched the snapshot years. The Joseon king list page did not return reign dates, so Korean reigns rest on my own knowledge (no discrepancies suspected).
