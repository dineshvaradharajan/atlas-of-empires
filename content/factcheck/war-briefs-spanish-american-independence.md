# War brief: Spanish American Wars of Independence (South America)

File: `content/war-briefs/spanish-american-wars-of-independence.json`.

- **Figures:** patriots c. 130,000 and Spain c. 30,000 troops sent, both from the infobox of Wikipedia's "Spanish
  American wars of independence". Both cover all of Spanish America, Mexico included, so `strengthWhat` says
  "across all Spanish America" and "troops sent from Spain"; no comparable figure exists for South America alone.
  Most royalist soldiers were Spanish Americans (same article), which the `who` line says.
- **Leaders:** Bolívar, San Martín, Sucre; Morillo, la Serna, Boves.
- **Result:** patriot victory; Ayacucho (9 Dec 1824) and Callao (23 Jan 1826) from the battle and siege articles.
- **Strategies:** Boves's llanero war (José Tomás Boves; chapter `second-republic`), the Andes crossings (Crossing of
  the Andes, Simón Bolívar; chapter `boyaca`), the continental strategy against Peru (Liberating Expedition of Peru,
  Military career of Simón Bolívar; chapter `junin-ayacucho`).
- **Impact:** Spain kept only Cuba and Puerto Rico until 1898 (moment `latin-american-independence`); the new
  republics; Gran Colombia's break-up by 1831 (Gran Colombia; the map's own note); Bolívar's conditional freeing of
  enslaved men who joined his armies (Military career of Simón Bolívar, decrees of 2 June and 16 July 1816) and his
  promise to Pétion (Simón Bolívar).
- **Not pinned down:** strength figures for South America alone; the number of British and Irish volunteers who
  served in South America (the infobox's 6,500 is for the whole wars), so no figure is given.
- **Errors noticed elsewhere:** none in `content/wars` or `content/cinema` beyond what the storyboard log records.
