# Lead images: europe slice

36 of 37 empires have a lead image. All files are from Wikimedia Commons; licence and author are as stated on each Commons file page (read via the API), and every image URL returned HTTP 200 with an image content type. Each thumbnail was opened and looked at before use. Dates in captions are the commonly given construction or creation dates and were checked from memory against the standard Wikipedia articles, not re-fetched (the API was heavily rate-limited), so a quick second look at the dates marked "check" is worthwhile.

## No image

- **Hunnic Empire**: nothing qualified. The Huns left no monuments, and the Commons files found were either maps and modern illustrations, or a modern copy of a Hunnic cauldron (a 2006 replica in a Kazan museum, blurry, portrait shape), or replicas of the Törtel cauldron. Real finds (cauldrons, diadems) were not available as suitable landscape photos in the searches run.
