## Unverifiable / disputed
- Peak area: no Taagepera or Turchin figure exists for the Pallavas. 100,000 km² follows this atlas's Cliopatria polygons (c. 104,000–105,000 km² for 602–805); Cliopatria's c. 305,000 km² for 306–352 was not used as it is not supported by the narrative sources. Peak year 650 (after the 642 sack of Vatapi) is a judgement, hedged in the note as a rough estimate.
- Reign dates vary by scholar (e.g. Mahendravarman I 590/600–630, Narasimhavarman II 690/695/700–728, Nandivarman II 718/731/732–796); all hedged with "c.".
- Simhavishnu's reign is disputed (555–590 / 575–600 / 575–615); the note says so.
- Nandivarman III: Wikipedia dates the Battle of Tellaru to 830, inconsistent with his 846–869 reign; the date was left out.
- Start year 275 follows Wikipedia; the map's Cliopatria realm runs 283–805.
