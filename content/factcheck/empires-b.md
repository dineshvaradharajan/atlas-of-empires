# Fact-check log: empires/b-late.json (states founded 1400 CE or later)

Method: each profile's dates, capital, rulers' reigns, peak area/year, summary and legacy were checked against fetched pages. Britannica, Encyclopaedia Iranica, the British Museum and most other tier-1 sites returned HTTP 403 to automated fetching, so the main sources are English Wikipedia articles (claims checked against their cited statements), Wikipedia's "List of largest empires" for the Taagepera (1979, 1997) and Turchin, Adams & Hall (2006) figures, World History Encyclopedia for basics, and the US Office of the Historian. Every profile now has a `sources` array. Peak notes now give the scholarly figure and who it comes from wherever one exists. Notes on profiles with no scholarly figure keep their "rough estimate" hedge and say that no figure was found.

## Unverifiable / disputed
- Portuguese Empire peak: kept the nominal 10.4M km² (1815, Brzezinski 2012) because it counts all of Brazil. The note now also gives Taagepera's c. 5.5M km² (1820), which counts only land Portugal actually controlled.
- Habsburg Monarchy peak 676,000 km²: no source found for that exact figure. EB1911 gives 621,538 km² for 1905, before Bosnia was added; with Bosnia the total is about 673,000 km². Kept as "c.".
- Soviet Union: kept the official 22.4M km²; Taagepera gives 22.3M km² for 1945. Republic of China: kept the 1946 nominal 9.67M km², and the note now gives the 1912 claim (11.4M km²) and Taagepera's 7.7M km².
- No scholarly area figure was found for Nader Shah's empire (3.0M km²), the Kazakh, Crimean, Bukharan, Omani, Toungoo, Malacca, Tokugawa and Saadian figures, or the Mutapa figure (Wikipedia's own 700,000 km² is tagged dubious). All keep their "c." or "rough estimate" hedges.
- Zulu Kingdom 30,000 km² core: unsourced. Gluckman (1960) gives about 80,000 sq mi for the wider area Shaka dominated by 1822; both are now in the note.
- Boer republics: area sources disagree (Wikipedia infoboxes give c. 191,800 km² for the Transvaal and 181,300 km² for the Orange Free State; older figures are higher for the Transvaal and lower for the Free State). The note now gives ranges.
- Sokoto Caliphate: estimates run from 400,000 to 488,500 km². Kept 470,000 as "About".
- Kazakh Khanate end date 1847 (Kenesary's death) kept. Wikipedia's infobox ends the khanate in 1780, but Kenesary's khanship (1841–1847) is well attested.
- Osei Bonsu's accession is dated 1800, 1801 or 1804 depending on the source; now shown as "c. 1800–1824".
- Conquest of the Desert: the text says 1878–85; Wikipedia's infobox has "1870s–1884". Not changed, since the main campaigns and the last surrender (Sayhueque, January 1885) fit the text.
- Bayinnaung's "largest empire in Southeast Asian history": kept, as Lieberman (2003, cited by Wikipedia) says so; the Toungoo dynasty article hedges it with "probably".
