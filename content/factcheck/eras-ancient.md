# Fact-check log: eras/a-ancient.json (3000 BCE – 1 BCE)

Checked every chapter's overview, 12 region texts, powers (ruler, capital, extent) and events against fetched pages. Britannica, the Met Heilbrunn Timeline, UCL Digital Egypt and the British Museum returned HTTP 403/429 to automated fetches, so citations are English Wikipedia articles (statements cited there) plus one UCLouvain academic entry. Chronologies used: Middle Chronology for Mesopotamia/Hatti, conventional (Shaw-style) Egyptian dates, Xia–Shang–Zhou Chronology Project dates for Zhou. Every chapter now has a `sources` array (6 entries each).

Note: one event `year` value was changed (2000 BCE chapter, Mentuhotep II event: -2055 → -2040) because the brief asked for event years to be checked; snapshot `year`, `id`, `map` and region keys were not touched.

## Unverifiable / disputed (left as is or only hedged)

- 1500 BCE: Mitanni king Parattarna (c. 1510–1490 BCE, Middle Chronology) and Kassite Burna-Buriash I (c. 1500 BCE) are plausible for the snapshot year but both reigns are poorly fixed; kept with "c.".
- 1500 BCE: Thutmose I's conquest of Kerma — Kerma was absorbed into New Kingdom Egypt c. 1500 BCE, but the final destruction is sometimes credited to later Thutmosid campaigns; kept.
- 1000 BCE: David's historicity and the size of the united monarchy are disputed; hedged (see corrections) rather than removed.
- 700 BCE: order of the Kushite kings Shabaka and Shebitku is debated; text already says so.
- 500 BCE: Chavín "at its peak" — Burger's chronology puts the Chavín horizon's spread c. 500–250 BCE, but newer work at Chavín de Huántar has the site's religious role ending before 400 BCE; kept, disputed.
- 500/400 BCE: Gojoseon and Zhangzhung as states at these dates rest largely on later traditional texts (Zhangzhung c. 500 BCE–625 CE per Wikipedia); kept as written.
- 500 BCE: Prasenajit of Kosala and Bimbisara are dated only through Buddhist chronology (long vs short); Bimbisara hedged.
- 300 BCE: Library and Museum of Alexandria founding "c. 295 BCE" — attributed to Ptolemy I or II; date approximate, kept with "c.".
- 200 BCE: Parthian capital at Hecatompylos (vs Nisa/Dara) is uncertain for Arsaces II; kept.
- 100 BCE: Parthian capital "Ctesiphon and Ecbatana" — Ctesiphon's status as capital under Mithridates II is not firmly dated; kept.
- 1 BCE: Kujula Kadphises unites the Kushans c. 30 CE, so "about to" is loose; Funan, Goguryeo/Silla/Baekje and Arakan dates are traditional; kept.
- 3000 BCE: Tasmania isolated "since about 10,000 BCE" — Wikipedia gives both c. 11,700 and c. 10,000 years ago; kept.
- 2000 BCE: Catacomb culture (c. 2500–1950 BCE) is at its very end in 2000 BCE; kept.
