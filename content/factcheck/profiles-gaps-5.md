## Unverifiable / disputed
- Hans (John) of Denmark: kept "1482 – 1513". Wikipedia's infobox gives 1482 (acclaimed spring 1482) while the Danish monarchs navbox gives 1481 (Christian I died 21 May 1481). Either is defensible.
- Stockholm Bloodbath "nearly 100": Wikipedia's lead says nearly 100; the executioner's own count was 82. Kept, but "more than 80" would be the stricter figure.
- Muhammad Ali's birthplace (Kavala vs a village in Ottoman Albania): disputed between references; the wording now avoids it.
- 1841 settlement and Sudan: Wikipedia says hereditary rule over Egypt and Sudan; LoC says Egypt hereditary, with Sudan kept. The text was softened to fit both.
- Novgorod's eastern limit (the northern Urals versus beyond them) varies by source; the note's "to the northern Urals" follows Encyclopedia.com.
- Britannica pages refused automated fetches (403); Britannica's 1056–1147 Almoravid span is taken from a search-result summary only, so it isn't cited.
