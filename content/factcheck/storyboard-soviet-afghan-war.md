# Storyboard: Soviet–Afghan War (`content/cinema/soviet-afghan-war.json`)

Sides: **0 = Soviet Union and the Afghan communist government, 1 = Afghan mujahideen**. Span 24 Dec 1979 – 31 Jul 1989.

## Chapters and why
1. **The Soviet invasion, December 1979**: 40th Army crossing on 25 Dec, the Termez–Kabul road, Storm-333 (Tajbeg Palace, 27 Dec).
2. **The Panjshir offensives** (Apr 1980 – Sep 1984): Massoud's valley; Panjshir V bases (Rukha, Bazarak, Anava), the 1983 truce (only Anava), Panjshir VII and the withdrawal to the lower valley.
3. **Zhawar and the first Stingers** (Feb–Sep 1986): second battle of Zhawar; first Stinger kill near Jalalabad on 25 Sep 1986.
4. **Operation Magistral** (Nov 1987 – Feb 1988): the Gardez–Khost road opened (Sata-Kandow pass, Khost entered 30 Dec) and abandoned at the end of January.
5. **The Geneva Accords and the Soviet withdrawal** (Apr 1988 – Feb 1989).
6. **Jalalabad, 1989** (Mar–Jul 1989): the failed mujahideen assault after the Soviets left.

## Areas and how they were made
- No front lines. The government/Soviets held cities and main roads, the mujahideen much of the countryside; the text says so.
- **Massoud's Panjshir valley** (side 1): the Panjshir province outline (geoBoundaries ADM1) minus a buffer along the valley floor for each phase (Panjshir V: mouth to beyond Bazarak; truce: only around Anava; Panjshir VII: whole valley to Paryan; Sept 1984: lower valley only). Valley line traced from Gulbahar–Anava–Rukha–Bazarak–Paryan; buffers 4.5–8 km.
- **Base-map gap fixed:** from 1987 the base map draws the whole country as `Mujahideen`, though Najibullah's government held the cities until 1992. Side-0 areas are added from 1 Jan 1987 for Kabul–Bagram, Kandahar, Herat, Mazar-i-Sharif, Kunduz, Ghazni, Shindand, Jalalabad, Khost and Gardez (circles/hand rings of 10–18 km; schematic). The Gardez area extends along the road to Khost on 2–30 Dec 1987 and pulls back by 5 Feb 1988; Jalalabad loses its south-eastern sector (airfield, Samarkhel) on 7–8 Mar 1989 and regains it by mid-July (dates of the Samarkhel recapture are "by June/July" in the source, so mid-July is approximate).

## Uncertainties
- Enclave sizes are schematic; roads between government towns were contested and are not drawn.
- The invasion arrow follows only the sourced Termez–Kabul road; the western column (Kushka–Herat–Kandahar) is not drawn because the fetched sources do not describe it.
- Bazarak (Wikipedia coordinates) is used for all Panjshir claims; Rukha and Anava positions are approximate and not used for claims.
- Soviet dead are given in the text as about 14,500; battle figures are claims (Zhawar, Magistral, Jalalabad).

## Warnings left (0 errors, 4 warnings)
- "Little visible change" in Panjshir, Magistral, withdrawal and Jalalabad chapters: their areas shrink and grow back within the chapter (net change small by design) or, for the withdrawal, are held unchanged because no dated loss of a mapped town is sourced.
