# Journeys log: slice B, medieval empires (`content/journeys/b-medieval.json`)

Method: every chain realm was checked against `public/data/clio/index.json`. Each chain entry lies inside the realm's years and has non-zero area rows for at least 97% of its from–to range (most 100%). Each span starts and ends inside its chain's coverage, and all events and reigns fall inside the span. Britannica (HTTP 403) and the Met's Heilbrunn Timeline (HTTP 429) refused automated fetches, so the sources are English Wikipedia articles and lists, each fetched and checked for the dates used, plus World History Encyclopedia pages for basic cross-checks. Where WHE and Wikipedia disagree, the event is hedged or left out (noted below). `content/corrections.json` notes for the Huns, Tang, Tibet, Abbasids, Chola, Khmer, Mongols, Inca and the Delhi Sultanate were read and respected. As in slice C, where the map starts after the true founding, the span starts with the map. The summary then gives the real date, and the first reign is clipped to the span with its true dates in its name (e.g. "Gaozu (r. 618–626)"). The array is in chronological order of span start, as JOURNEYS.md requires, not in the order of the brief's list.

## Per empire
- **hunnic-empire**: `Huns` 370–425 → `Hunnic Empire` 426–469. This follows the corrections.json clip (370–469). The map's 451 peak (1.9M km²) is about half the c. 4.0M km² of Taagepera 1979. Rugila's death is 434 in Wikipedia's Attila article and 433 in WHE. The Treaty of Margus is 435 in Wikipedia ("the following year") and 439 in WHE; 435 is used. Nedao is hedged "c. 454". Uldin's dates are approximate (first named c. 400, last active 408).
- **byzantine-empire**: `Eastern Roman Empire` 395–632 → `Byzantine Empire` 633–1205 → `Nicaean Empire` 1206–1278 → `Byzantine Empire` 1279–1453. The middle stage is needed because the `Byzantine Empire` realm has only a tiny 4k km² polygon for 1206–26 and no rows for 1227–78. Nicaea stands in for the empire in exile and covers the 1261 recapture. The `Byzantine Empire` realm has no rows for 1402–06. The Vandal war is given as 533–534 (Wikipedia: late 533; WHE: 534). Heraclius' victory is dated 627 (Nineveh), as Wikipedia gives it. The Ottoman advance into Europe is shown by Adrianople, 1369 (the article says the expansion began in 1354 but names no place for it).
- **tang-dynasty**: `Tang Dynasty` 623–907. Founded 618, but Cliopatria starts in 623 (corrections.json), so Gaozu's reign is clipped. The map draws the 661 peak (8.7M km²) larger than Taagepera's 5.4M km² (715). Gaozong's end year (683) and Zhaozong's start (888) are standard but were not in the fetched text.
- **tibetan-empire**: `Tibetan Empire` 623–842. The map starts in 623 and is clipped at 842 per corrections.json; Songtsen Gampo's reign (c. 618–650) is clipped. The conquest of Zhangzhung is disputed (634, 645, or under Trisong Detsen), so it is hedged "c. 645". The Kabul submission is hedged "c. 812" (the article gives 812 or 815). Sadnalegs' reign is given as 800–815 (the Blue Annals give 814). The reign of Muné Tsenpo (797–799) is omitted as disputed.
- **rashidun-umayyad**: `Rashidun Caliphate` 633–660 → `Umayyad Caliphate` 661–750. Cliopatria starts the Rashidun in 633 (Abu Bakr's reign, 632–634, is clipped). It also starts the Umayyad realm in 656, but its 656–660 rows are small (0.39M km², apparently Mu'awiya's Syria), so the hand-over is set at 661. The Umayyad area c. 11.1–15M km² (720) in the summary is from Wikipedia's infobox ("c. 11 million"); the map peaks at 9.9M km². Jerusalem is dated April 637 (other accounts say 638). Alexandria's surrender is dated by the treaty of 8 Nov 641 (Egypt complete by 642). The journey stops at 750; the Umayyad emirate of Córdoba (756) appears only in the Abbasid journey.
- **abbasid-caliphate**: `Abbasid Caliphate` 750–969 → `Abbasid Caliphate` 1202–1258. Gap: the realm has no rows for 970–1201, the Buyid and Seljuk era when the caliphs held no territory of their own. So the span runs 750–1258 but draws nothing for those 232 years, and the 1055 and 1180 events fall in the gap. The overlapping `Abbasid Caliphate/Buyid Dynasty` (947–969) was not used. The map's peak (8.2M km², 778) is below the scholarly 11.1M km² (corrections.json). The Ibn Tulun event is hedged "c. 870" (the article says "by the 870s"). WHE does not date Baghdad's founding (Wikipedia: 762).
- **carolingian-empire**: `(Carolingian Empire)` 751–888. The composite group was chosen, not the one-row `Carolingian Empire` realm (718–723 only). The span is Pepin's kingship (751) to the death of Charles the Fat (888); he was deposed in 887. WHE gives Louis the Pious as r. 813–840 (he was co-emperor from 813); 814 is used for his sole rule. After 843 the composite still draws all the Frankish kingdoms together.
- **ghana-empire**: `Ghana Empire` 793–1201. Gaps: Cliopatria draws Ghana with one fixed outline of about 204k km² for its whole run (no growth or decline). The span cannot show the empire's earlier origins (possibly 6th century) or the Sosso conquest of c. 1203. The Almoravid conquest of 1076 is disputed by modern scholars (Conrad and Fisher 1982). WHE even dates al-Bakri's account to 1076; Wikipedia gives c. 1068, which is used. **Only 3 reign entries**: Bassi (d. 1063) and Tunka Manin (from 1063) are the only kings known by name, so the rest are grouped as "names unrecorded". There are 8 events, the minimum.
- **khmer-empire**: `Khmer Empire` 802–1431. The polygons from 1432 are relabelled as post-Angkor Cambodia (corrections.json), so the span ends in 1431. The map's peak (953k km², 1206–19) is close to Taagepera's 1.0M km² (1290). Jayavarman VII's reign ends in 1218 (his article); the Khmer Empire article says 1219 and WHE says 1215. WHE dates the start of Angkor Wat to c. 1122. The Koh Ker move (Jayavarman IV, c. 928) was left out because no date was given.
- **chola-empire**: `Chola Empire` 850–1259. Cliopatria starts in 850 (Vijayalaya's founding c. 848 is in the summary and his reign is clipped). It ends in 1259 with only 8k km², so Rajendra III and the final collapse (c. 1279) appear only in the summary. The map is tiny before 1000 (3–131k km²). Sri Lanka's Rajarata is dated "c. 1017" (Mahavamsa: 1017–18). Wikipedia names the Pandya victor of 1215–16 as Maravarman Sundara Pandyan II, but he is usually counted as I, so the event text gives no regnal number. Vijayalaya's end and Aditya I's start (871) are standard but were not in the fetched text.
- **holy-roman-empire**: `(Holy Roman Empire)` 962–1806, the composite group (the plain `Holy Roman Empire` realm has no rows for 1260–1618). The journey runs to 1806 because the empire's whole life was asked for; it goes well past the medieval slice. Data gap: the composite falls from c. 0.9M km² to c. 0.35M km² after the 1440s, at the point where Cliopatria's vassalage and allegiance groups for Bohemia (to 1452), Brandenburg and the Swiss Confederation (to 1458) end. This seems to be a mapping artefact, not a real territorial loss. Reigns use imperial-reign dates (coronation or election as emperor) from Wikipedia's list; Rudolf I is shown as king (never crowned). The span of the interregnum differs between sources (1245/1250/1254–1273, or to 1312), so the text says only that it "follows".
- **seljuk-empire**: `Seljuk Dynasty` 1037–1039 → `Great Seljuk Empire` 1040–1194. The Sultanate of Rum is not drawn (it has its own realm). The map's peak (4.1M km², 1085–93) matches Wikipedia's 3.9M km². The capture of Merv and Nishapur is dated 1037–38. The 1194 end follows the article (Toghrul III defeated by Tekish); the battle site near Rayy was not confirmed, so that event has no lonlat.
- **mongol-empire**: `Mongol Empire` 1206–1293. Cliopatria ends the united realm in 1293, so the span stops there and Kublai's reign is clipped. His death in 1294 and the four successor khanates (Yuan, Golden Horde, Chagatai Khanate, Ilkhanate) are in the summary. Cliopatria starts the separate khanate realms only in 1294 (corrections.json), and they are not part of this chain. The map's peak (27.4M km², 1279) exceeds Turchin et al.'s c. 24M km². WHE dates the start of the Khwarazm war to 1218 (Wikipedia: 1219–21) and calls the khanates autonomous from 1260; 1219 is used.
- **delhi-sultanate**: `(Delhi Sultanate)` 1210–1526. Founded in 1206, but the composite starts in 1210, so Aibak's reign is clipped to a single year. Corrections.json notes the dispute over the peak: Taagepera and Turchin put it in 1312 (3.2M km²), while the map and Schwartzberg put it in the 1330s. The composite has no Sayyid-era extent beyond 89k km² (1407–52). Daulatabad is dated 1327 (its article). The Gujarat event uses 1299 (raided), with annexation in 1304.
- **mali-empire**: `Mali Empire` 1236–1610. Kirina (c. 1235) comes one year before the map, so it is in the summary and Sundiata's reign is clipped. The end is hedged: Wikipedia says "the end came in 1599" and the empire broke up after Mahmud IV's death c. 1610. WHE is internally inconsistent (1240–1645, "collapsed in the 1460s"). Reign dates follow Wikipedia's Keita dynasty list; the early ones are "c." dates. Whether Gao and Timbuktu submitted to Musa in 1325 is doubted by the Musa I article, so the event says only that he built in Timbuktu (hedged "c. 1325"). Takeover of Ghana's capital hedged "c. 1240" (WHE).
- **aztec-empire**: `Tenochtitlan` 1326–1428 → `Aztec Triple Alliance` 1429–1520. The traditional founding date of 1325 (Wikipedia) is in the summary only; WHE gives 1345 as the "traditional date", so the founding is disputed. The fall of Tenochtitlan (13 Aug 1521) comes after the map's last year and is in the summary only; Cuauhtémoc's reign is clipped. Moctezuma I's death is 1468 in Wikipedia's Aztec Empire article and 1469 in WHE (the tlatoque list gives 1466); 1469 is used. Tenochtitlan's realm is only about 2k km², so the map shows a city until 1429.
- **timurid-empire**: `Timurid Empire` 1375–1507. Timur took power c. 1370 at Balkh; the map starts in 1375. Wikipedia says Isfahan fell in 1387, but WHE dates a different 1387 event (Van). The Golden Horde victory is dated 1395 (WHE: Terek, 14 Apr 1395; Wikipedia: 1394–95) and has no lonlat. Timur's death at Farab/Otrar is dated Feb 1405. The span ends in 1507 with the fall of Herat; the realm continues to 1528 for Babur's Kabul state, which is not part of this journey. Only 5 reign entries (the main rulers); there were many rival regional Timurid rulers after 1449.
- **inca-empire**: `Inca Empire` 1440–1533. Cliopatria starts in 1440; Pachacuti's traditional reign (1438–71, Rowe's chronology) is clipped and labelled as traditional. Radiocarbon work suggests an earlier start. The 1534–39 polygon is hidden by corrections.json, and the map under-draws the empire before 1497. Chimor's conquest is hedged "c. 1470" (WHE timeline). Huayna Capac's death is "c. 1527" (Wikipedia 1527; WHE 1525/1526/1528). Vilcabamba (to 1572) is in the summary only. Only 8 events, because dated pre-conquest events are scarce.
- **songhai-empire**: `Songhai Empire` 1464–1591. The realm's polygons are 0 km² after 1595. Djenné is dated 1473 (Wikipedia's Songhai and Sonni Ali articles and WHE); one sentence of the Songhai article says 1475. Timbuktu is dated 1468 (WHE: 1469). The hajj is given as "1496 or 1497–98" (both appear in the Askia Muhammad I article). Askia Muhammad's accession is 1493 (WHE: 1494).

## Summary
19 journeys, none with an empty chain. All chain names exist in index.json, and ids, spans, events (8–15 each), reigns and sources (3–6 each) pass validation. Notable gaps: the Abbasid chain has no map rows for 970–1201. Ghana is drawn with one fixed outline and has only 3 reign entries, as few of its kings are known. The Chola and Mongol spans stop before their true ends (1279, 1294) because the map stops. The Holy Roman Empire composite shrinks artificially after the 1440s. The Byzantine journey needs the Nicaean Empire for 1206–78.
