## No image

- **Ghana Empire (Wagadu)** (`ghana-empire`): No Commons file of Koumbi Saleh or Wagadu works qualified (only satellite views, diagrams and maps).
- **Kanem-Bornu Empire** (`kanem-bornu`): Nothing found on Commons beyond flags, coats of arms and maps.
- **Kingdom of Kongo** (`kingdom-of-kongo`): Only portrait-format Kongo crucifixes found (Commons crop would cut them); no landscape or square image qualified.
- **Thule culture** (`thule-culture`): Searches returned no usable Commons file.
- **Sokoto Caliphate** (`sokoto-caliphate`): Only modern photos of the Sultan's palace, which is not a work of the caliphate era; nothing qualified.
- **Zulu Kingdom** (`zulu-kingdom`): Results were colonial-era photographs or cartoons of Zulu people, not works made by the Zulu kingdom; nothing qualified.
- **Kingdom of Mutapa** (`mutapa`): Only a 17th-century European painting and a coat-of-arms image; not made by the Mutapa state.
- **Merina Kingdom (Madagascar)** (`merina-kingdom`): Available Rova photos were a distant hillside view, an over-processed snapshot, or the restored (post-1995 fire) palace.
- **Republic of Liberia** (`liberia`): Only portrait-format portraits and procession photos; nothing landscape or square from the period.
- **Gran Colombia** (`gran-colombia`): Bolivar portraits by Gil de Castro are portrait format only (crop would lose the subject).
