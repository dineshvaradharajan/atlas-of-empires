# Cambrian story image prompts

## Scientific corrections

The six edited images retain the original scenes while correcting these details:

- `new-gaskiers.png`: remove all large Ediacaran organisms from the 580-million-year-old coast.
- `new-ediacara.png`: replace the legged Spriggina-like figures with flat, soft-bodied, quilted forms.
- `new-sirius-passet.png`: give Kerygmachela separate lateral flaps and a single long tail spine.
- `new-treptichnus.png`: show an irregular series of linked burrow segments with mostly upward projections.
- `new-burgess.png`: reconstruct Pikaia without visible eyes, with head tentacles and muscle blocks.
- `new-timeline.png`: show a bare ice-age coast and a later Cambrian seafloor with trilobites and sponges.

These prompts describe generated reconstructions. Colors and details not preserved in fossils are interpretive.
